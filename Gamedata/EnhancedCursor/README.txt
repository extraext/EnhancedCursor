=====================
EnhancedCursor v1.7.0
=====================

FEATURES:
- Cursor & Camera control enhancements
- Idle cursor auto-hide during flight
- Customizable cursor highlight halo
- Full UI settings window (AppLauncher button)
- Custom cursors

and more.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALLATION:
1. Open the .zip file.
2. Drag and drop the "EnhancedCursor" folder in Gamedata/EnhancedCursor into your KSP directory's gamedata folder.
3. Ensure the final path is: KSP/GameData/EnhancedCursor
---------------
DEPENDENCIES:
1. Harmony2: Ensure it's installed in your Gamedata folder.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY:
- Built for KSP 1.8-1.12.
- Compatible with other camera-related mods.
